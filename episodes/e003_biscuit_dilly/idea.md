# Episode 003 — Biscuit, Poppy & Dilly (the dog heartworm)

*Dirofilaria immitis*, the dog heartworm; a female mosquito; a dog.
Owner's idea (2026-10-30): a happy dog on a sunny afternoon, a mosquito bite, and the heartworm's journey from the
skin to the lung arteries, ending on a life-cycle image like e001's.

## Cast
- **Biscuit** — dog (male). New. Stays calm and fine-looking on screen (owner: no suffering, no medical talk).
- **Poppy** — mosquito (female: only females bite). New.
- **Dilly** — dog heartworm (female; we follow her from larva to adult). New. Name from *Diro*filaria.

## Hook (owner picked option 2)
Teaser + first line: **"This worm grows up inside two animals: a dog... and a mosquito."**
(First approved as "This worm can't grow up without a dog and a mosquito"; judge pass 1 showed that overstates the
host range — coyotes, foxes, wolves, sometimes cats — and the owner approved the reworded hook and closing line
"A dog. A mosquito. And a worm that lives in both.")

## The story in beats
1. **Hook.** Biscuit dozes in the sun. Poppy drifts down.
2. **Meet them.** Biscuit, then Poppy: only females bite, they need blood for their eggs. Inside her rides Dilly,
   picked up as a baby from another dog's blood; she grew in Poppy for about two weeks, in warm weather, and
   crawled to the mouthparts.
3. **The bite.** (sfx, not spoken.) Close-up: Dilly isn't injected. She spills onto the skin in a tiny drop and
   wriggles in through the bite.
4. **The long walk.** A molt within a week or two, then about two months wandering under the skin and through muscle.
5. **The ride.** A final molt into a young worm; into a vein; carried to the heart.
6. **The turn.** "She's called a heartworm… but she mostly lives in the arteries of the lungs." Arrives about
   three or four months after the bite, an inch long; grows about tenfold.
7. **Grown up.** As long as a ruler (~30 cm); males shorter. Six months or more after the bite, she and a male have
   babies that drift in Biscuit's blood. Biscuit looks fine on screen.
8. **The problem.** The babies can't grow up inside Biscuit. They need a mosquito.
9. **The cycle.** Evening: a mosquito comes for a sip, the babies go up into her mouthparts, she flies off.
   The closing line names the three of them over the meadow, the teaser's lens returns with Dilly in it.
   End card: *Dirofilaria immitis* / the dog heartworm, and the logo. (A cycle diagram was tried in the look
   frames; the owner dropped it, 2026-10-30.)

## Owner decisions
- No prevention / medicine line ("we are here to present the teeny tiny creatures, not medical stuff").
- No "L3/L4" in the narration; the stage labels appear on the cycle card only.
