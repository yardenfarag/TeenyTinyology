## Hook and closing line (judge pass 1)
Never "can't grow up without a dog" / "needs a dog": coyotes, foxes and wolves are normal hosts, cats occasional
(Merck, AHS, Wikipedia). The narration describes Dilly's own story instead: "This worm grows up inside two animals:
a dog… and a mosquito." / "A dog. A mosquito. And a worm that lives in both." (owner approved, 2026-10-30).

Not claimed: number of babies per day; exact male length; the meaning of *immitis*.
