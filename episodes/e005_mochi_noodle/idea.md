# Episode 005 — Mochi, Pip & Noodle (the flea tapeworm)

*Dipylidium caninum* (the flea tapeworm / "cucumber-seed tapeworm"), passed between a flea and a cat.
Owner's idea (2026-11-11): "a remake for the cat Mochi and the flea and worm" — the original cast's story, told
with today's rules (illustrated style, Miles, judge loop). No earlier version exists in the repo.

## Cast (all existing)
- **Mochi** — cat (proposed: female). Backlog: redesign her muzzle before this episode's look frames.
- **Pip** — flea (proposed: male): larva → cocoon → adult. Cat flea, *Ctenocephalides felis*.
- **Noodle** — tapeworm, *Dipylidium caninum* (proposed: "she"; really a hermaphrodite, every segment has both
  sets of organs — not mentioned in narration).

## Hook
Teaser + first line: **"There's a grain of rice on this cat's fur. And it's moving."** (CDC/CAPC: fresh segments crawl;
dried ones look like rice grains.)

## The story in beats
1. **Hook.** A tiny cream grain on Mochi's fur, inching along.
2. **Meet Mochi; reveal the grain.** A piece of a tapeworm, full of eggs. It dries, breaks open, eggs spill into the bed.
3. **Pip the larva.** A baby flea in the bedding eats crumbs — and a tapeworm egg.
4. **Inside Pip.** The egg hatches, the young worm moves into his body and waits (quiescent in the pupa).
5. **Grown-up Pip.** Cocoon → adult → jumps onto Mochi. The warmth of her skin finishes the worm (Pugh 1987).
6. **Itch… lick… gulp.** Mochi grooms and swallows Pip.
7. **Noodle.** Freed in Mochi's gut, she anchors with hooks and four suckers, has no mouth, soaks up food through her
   skin, grows segment after segment.
8. **The cycle.** Ripe segments break off, crawl out onto the fur — grains of rice, waiting for the next Pip.
9. **Calm outro.** Most cats barely notice; a vet can clear it; stopping fleas stops the next one.
   End card: *Dipylidium caninum* · flea tapeworm · logo plate.
