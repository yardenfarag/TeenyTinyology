# E005 "Mochi, Pip & Noodle" — judge report, pass 1

Video: `episodes/e005_mochi_noodle/out/005-mochi-pip-and-noodle_illustrated.mp4` (90.86 s). Packet: `review/judge/packet.md`.

## Verdict
Clear, warm, accurate; the full cycle reads in one watch, every narrated fact checks out. Weak spots are visual and
mostly repeats of known lessons: three narrated actions without payoff (hooks never hold the gut, no segment breaks
off, the swallow isn't shown), a "Pip" tag pointing at an egg packet before Pip is on screen, a 30 cm ruler inside the
gut that makes the gut ~35 cm wide, and five labels under 2 s. No re-voice needed. Publishable after the Must-fix items.

Scorecard: Facts 9 · Science visuals 6 · Story clarity 8 · Visual polish 7 · UI/UX 8 · Pacing & audio 8 · Overall 7

## Must fix
1. 21.0–22.8 s: "Pip / a flea larva: no legs yet" tag points at an egg packet while the larva is still off screen
   (tag anchored to the larva's x, clamped to ≥ 200). Start the larva on screen or start the tag when he's in view.
2. 57.9–62.6 s: "holds on … with a crown of tiny hooks" while the scolex floats in open lumen, hooks touching nothing.
   Sink the head between villi, grip squash on "hooks", suckers pulse on "suckers". (Visual payoff, now ×6.)
3. 76.5–78.1 s: "The oldest ones break off" shows an unbroken worm; the tail is off frame. Frame the tail end and pop
   the last 1–2 segments free on "break"; centre the iris on the drifting segment.

## Should fix
4. 72.5–76.4 s: 30 cm ruler drawn inside the gut → gut reads ~35 cm wide (real: ~1 cm). Show ruler vs worm on a card/inset.
5. 54.5–62.5 s: Noodle arrives already long; then "she grows" shows growth already done. Start as scolex + neck + 1–3 segments.
6. Labels under 2 s: "dried: hard, like rice" ≈0.6 s, "Pip, grown up" ≈0.8 s, "adults: 10 to 70 cm" ≈1.1 s,
   "4 suckers" ≈1.4 s, "male + female parts" ≈1.5 s.
7. 30.4–37 s: the hatch is a 10 px ring. Add an x-ray lens: shell splits, six-hooked oncosphere wriggles out, slips through the gut wall.
8. 42.4–46.7 s: the worm "gets ready" as a 10 px ring; reads as a held frame. Bigger cysticercoid, glow brightening on
   "warm"/"ready", heat shimmer in the lens.
9. 51.0–53.8 s: "and swallows Pip" shows no swallow. Eyes close, chin dip, throat bob, lip lick.
10. 48.9–51 s: licking pose leaves an outlined cream hump behind the turned head; tongue hangs below the chin rather than on the flea point.
11. 78.12–78.99 s: "crawl out..." caption 0.87 s, straddling the iris. Move the "|".
12. 19–29 s: egg packets ~1/7 of larva length and 4× the crumbs (real: 120–200 µm vs 2–5 mm larva). Shrink to crumb size, one hero packet with a glint.

## Nice to have
13. Adult Pip reads as a tick/beetle (round, even legs); real fleas are laterally flattened with big hind legs.
14. 63–67 s: food dots drift but are never absorbed.
15. 47.1–48.9 s: "Pip's bite itches" has no bite (use `bite=`), no skin twitch.
16. Pip's intro skips the "This is <Name>…" pattern (approved narration — keep the pattern next time).
17. `boing` on "ruler" with nothing bouncing → `pop`/`sparkle`.
18. End card line "carried by the cat flea…" ~30 px < 34 px minimum.

## What works

the fresh segment → rice grain → egg packets sequence and the closing grains bookend; accurate legless flea larva and
cocoon; labels that add facts; scolex close-up drawing; one room camera; real iris overlaps; captions one line in the
safe zone; watermark clear; −14.0 LUFS, sample peak −1.75 dBFS, LRA 3.2 LU, no silence; end card readable ~5.3 s.
