## Description

I make short animated stories about tiny creatures: fleas, ants, crickets, mayflies, worms, and the bigger animals
they share their lives with.

Each video is a small true story from nature. I read up on the facts, then draw, animate and put together the music
and sounds from scratch. They take a while to make, and I enjoy every part of it.

I'd love to share them with you. New ones whenever they're ready.
