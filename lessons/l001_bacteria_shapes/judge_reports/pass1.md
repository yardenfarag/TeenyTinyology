# L001 judge report — pass 1 (2026-10-27)

## Verdict
A strong pilot that genuinely teaches: after one watch a curious adult can name cocci/bacilli/spirals, tell a chain
from a cluster, and decode *Staphylococcus aureus* as "golden grape-bunch berry". The six-region board reads as a real
map and the facts are well hedged. One closing line is false as written and should be fixed before publishing (w1
calls the threads "single cells"; the threads are *Streptomyces*, which is not one cell). Beyond that the clear
improvements are: the rule behind the cocci groupings is only spoken, never drawn; two terms are undefined (flagella,
archaeon); two board labels misstate the surface-area point; the jingle sits ~4 LU louder than the format allows; and
the final map holds static for ~17 s at an unreadable size. Publishable after the one Must fix; the board-only
Should fixes are cheap and worth doing in the same pass.
